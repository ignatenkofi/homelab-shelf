## 13.10 Power On Reset (POR)

After power is applied, the X550 must be reset. There are two ways to do this:
1. Using the internal Power on Reset (POR) circuit with external LAN_PWR_GOOD signal pulled up.
2. Using the internal POR circuit with the external reset as LAN_PWR_GOOD signal generated by the
   system.
The power supply sequencing timing requirement between the 3.3V and 2.1V, 1.2V, and 0.83V power
rails has to be met (see Section 12.3.8).
There are two options for implementing POR:
1. Internal POR (recommended as default).
       a. BYPASS_POR signal (pull down).
       b. LAN_PWR_GOOD signal (pull up).
2. External reset with LAN_PWR_GOOD signal:
       a. BYPASS_POR signal (pull down).
       b. LAN_PWR_GOOD signal generated by the system following device power-up timing.
