Reset Network & Password
When the NAS device is turned on, use a pin-like object to insert and hold the "RESET" hole
for Ss until you hear the buzzer beeping. The device will not restart during the reset process.
After the reset is complete, the solid white network indicators will turn on, indicating that the
device is ready for use.
Please note that the reset will not affect the data stored in the hard drive. After successful
resetting, you can log in to the system with the "admin" account and blank password (this
accountis specifically utilized for password resets). Once logged in, follow the step-by-step
wizard to configure your administrator password.
